  landscape, followed by a second gradient correction. Replaces sky only.

  reconstruction of the original wisps. Generated alpha retained, lossless WebP.

  reconstruction of a small portion of the existing left tree. Alpha retained.
ambient-sky-region.png: 1672x877 numeric mask from original source RGB. For x>=200,
  first non-blue sky pixel below y170; left protected ridge y=280+.25*x.
  Two source-pixel safety gap, three-pixel feather. Lower green foliage remains
  stationary from y>.22*877. It is a conservative mask, not a depth reconstruction.
ambient-room-light.png: 512x320 RGB numeric receiving map authored for the
  unchanged 1585x992 room. R/G: cloud-plane receiving UV; B: direct-light fraction.
  Parallel-light receiver projection uses fixed upper-left cloud-plane sun UV
  [0.1764705882,0.0862745098], a designed artistic anchor, not a measurement.
  The attenuation reads the same moving cloud alpha; ambient base remains lit.
  This is not recovered physical irradiance, albedo or baked illumination.

at the 1440 review viewport. Branch free end is bounded near 3px, with a 6s main
period and a 24s shared gust; the lower root remains fixed. Branch and clouds
share the same exterior projection and visible-seconds clock.
